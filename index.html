I'm currently a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Connecticut. My research interests involve using applied mathematics and statistics to model social processes through time.

I've recently enjoyed thinking about how mixture models can be used to infer latent political cultures across time and space in US states and Swiss municipalities.
